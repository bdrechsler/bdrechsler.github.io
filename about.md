I am an astronomy PhD student and Reber Fellow at the University of Virginia. My research focuses on the earliest stage of star formation. I use data from state of the art facilities such as ALMA and JWST to study these objects, called protostars, to adress some key open questions:
  - By what mechanism are these objects gaining new material and growing. Is this accretion constant or variable in time?
  - How massive are these protostars? How does their distribution of masses compare to the IMF for main sequence stars?
  - When are the conditions in the disks around protostars favorable for planet formation?
